# ETF Tracker User Guide

Track and analyze Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) with real-time data and comprehensive analytics.

## What is the ETF Tracker?

The ETF Tracker is a tool that helps you monitor Exchange-Traded Funds, providing insights into performance, trends, and key metrics. Whether you're an investor, financial professional, or simply tracking the market, this tool gives you the data you need.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the ETF Tracker

1. Navigate to "ETF Tracker" from the main navigation menu
2. The tracker will load with a default view of popular ETFs
3. No login or account creation required

## Key Features

### 1. ETF Overview

View essential information about each ETF:
- **Name and Ticker Symbol**: Identify the fund
- **Current Price**: Real-time or recent pricing data
- **Performance Metrics**: Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly changes
- **Key Statistics**: Volume, market cap, and other relevant data

### 2. Performance Tracking

Monitor how ETFs perform over time:
- **Historical Data**: View past performance trends
- **Comparative Analysis**: Compare multiple ETFs side-by-side
- **Time Ranges**: Select different time periods for analysis

### 3. Search and Filter

Find specific ETFs quickly:
- **Search by Ticker**: Enter the ETF symbol (e.g., SPY, QQQ, VTI)
- **Search by Name**: Find ETFs by their full name
- **Filter by Category**: Browse ETFs by sector or type

## How to Use

### Viewing ETF Data

1. **Browse Available ETFs**: Scroll through the main list of tracked ETFs
2. **Click for Details**: Select an ETF to view detailed information
3. **Check Performance**: Review performance charts and statistics
4. **Compare ETFs**: Add multiple ETFs to your watchlist for comparison

### Understanding the Data

#### Price Information
- **Current Price**: The most recent trading price
- **Change**: Dollar amount change from previous close
- **Percentage Change**: Percentage change from previous close
- **Open/Close**: Opening and closing prices for the trading day

#### Performance Indicators
- **Day Range**: Highest and lowest prices during the trading day
- **52-Week Range**: Yearly high and low prices
- **Volume**: Number of shares traded
- **Average Volume**: Typical trading volume over a period

### Best Practices

3. **Long-Term View**: Consider long-term trends rather than daily fluctuations
4. **Research First**: Use this as one tool in your research process
5. **Stay Informed**: Understand what the ETF invests in before tracking

## Troubleshooting

### Data Not Loading
- Refresh your browser
- Check your internet connection
- Try clearing browser cache
- Wait a few moments if markets are experiencing high volume

### Incorrect or Outdated Data
- Verify the last update timestamp
- Check if markets are currently open
- Consider market holidays and closures
- Cross-reference with other sources if needed

### Can't Find a Specific ETF
- Verify the ticker symbol is correct
- Check if the ETF is actively traded
- Some newer or less common ETFs may not be included
- Try searching by full name instead of ticker

### Understanding Market Terms
- **ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)**: A fund that trades on stock exchanges
- **Ticker Symbol**: The unique abbreviation for the ETF (e.g., SPY)
- **NAV (Net Asset Value)**: The per-share value of the fund's holdings
- **Expense Ratio**: Annual fee charged by the fund
- **Dividend Yield**: Annual dividends divided by share price
