# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions and answers about using Platzer.dev.

## General Questions

### What is Platzer.dev?

Platzer.dev is a collection of useful tools bridging software development and coffee culture, created by Luis Platzer. It offers utilities for coffee professionals, investors, and general users.

### Do I need to create an account?

No, most features on the website are available without registration or login. Simply visit the site and start using the tools.

### Is the website free to use?

Yes, all tools are currently free to use. There are no subscription fees or hidden costs.

### What browsers are supported?

The website works on all modern browsers:
- Chrome (recommended)
- Firefox
- Safari
- Edge
- Opera

Mobile browsers are also supported.

### Is there a mobile app?

Currently, there is no dedicated mobile app, but the website is mobile-responsive and works well on smartphones and tablets.

## Coffee Tools Questions

### What is the SCA Flavour Wheel?

The SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) Flavour Wheel is the industry standard for describing coffee flavours and aromas. Our interactive version makes it easier to explore and use during coffee tastings.

### Do I need to be a coffee professional to use the coffee tools?

No! The tools are designed for both professionals and enthusiasts. Whether you're a barista, roaster, or just love coffee, these tools can help improve your coffee experience.

### Can I use the flavour wheel during cupping sessions?

Absolutely! That's one of the primary use cases. You can reference it on a laptop, tablet, or phone during professional cupping sessions or casual tastings.

### Will more coffee tools be added?

Yes, additional coffee tools are in development, including cupping sheets, brewing calculators, and water recipe builders.

### What if the data seems incorrect?

## QR Code Generator Questions

### What types of QR codes can I create?

Currently, the tool specializes in WiFi QR codes, allowing you to share network credentials easily. Additional QR code types may be added in the future.

### Is it safe to create WiFi QR codes?

Yes, the QR code generation happens in your browser. However, be mindful of where you share or post WiFi QR codes, as anyone who scans them can access your network.

### Can I customize the QR code appearance?

Standard QR codes are generated currently. Customization options may be added in future updates.

### Will the QR code work on all phones?

Most modern smartphones (iOS 11+ and Android 10+) can scan QR codes natively using the camera app. Older devices may need a dedicated QR reader app.

### Can I create QR codes for my business?

Yes! The WiFi QR code generator is perfect for businesses, offices, restaurants, hotels, and any venue where you want to share network access easily.
