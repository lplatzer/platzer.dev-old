# Coffee Tools Guide

This section covers all the coffee-related tools available on Platzer.dev, designed to help coffee professionals and enthusiasts improve their craft.

## Available Tools

### Interactive Coffee Flavour Wheel

An interactive version of the SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) Arabica Flavour Wheel.

#### What is it?

The Coffee Flavour Wheel is a comprehensive visual guide to the flavours and aromas found in coffee. It's based on the official SCA Arabica Flavour Wheel, which is the industry standard for coffee tasting and evaluation.

#### How to Use

1. **Navigate to the Tool**: Select "Interactive Coffee Flavour Wheel" from the Coffee Tools menu
2. **Explore Flavour Categories**: The wheel is organized in layers:
   - **Inner Circle**: Main flavour categories (fruity, sweet, floral, etc.)
   - **Middle Circle**: Sub-categories within each main category
   - **Outer Circle**: Specific flavour notes and descriptors
3. **Interact with the Wheel**: Click or hover over different sections to explore specific flavour notes
4. **Use During Cupping**: Reference the wheel while tasting coffee to identify and describe flavours

#### Best Practices

- **Start Broad, Go Specific**: Begin with the inner circle categories and work your way outward
- **Use Multiple Descriptors**: Coffee often has multiple flavour notes - don't limit yourself to one
- **Consider Context**: Flavours can vary based on brewing method, roast level, and origin
- **Practice Regularly**: The more you use the wheel, the better you'll become at identifying flavours

#### Who Should Use This?

- **Baristas**: Improve your ability to describe coffee to customers
- **Coffee Roasters**: Evaluate and communicate the flavour profiles of your roasts
- **Coffee Enthusiasts**: Develop your palate and coffee tasting skills
- **Coffee Shop Owners**: Train staff on flavour identification
- **Q Graders and Cuppers**: Reference during formal coffee evaluations

## Tips for Coffee Tasting

1. **Start with Clean Water**: Use filtered water for the most accurate taste
2. **Consistent Brewing**: Use the same brewing parameters each time you taste
3. **Take Notes**: Document the flavours you detect
4. **Compare and Contrast**: Taste multiple coffees side-by-side
5. **Stay Hydrated**: Cleanse your palate with water between tastings
